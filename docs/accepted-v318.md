## Fixed in v3.18 (for the record, not accepted)

- a3749636 medium, buy tax on unconsumed ETH: `beforeSwap` charged the stepped launch tax (90/50/25%) and the 1% share on the full exact-input amount, which v4 settles before the pool reports what it consumed, so a buy whose `sqrtPriceLimitX96` was reached (10 ETH specified, about 0.51 ETH swapped) paid 9 ETH of tax on ETH the pool never took, and a front-run could push any slippage-derived limit into that state. The hook cannot size an ETH charge in `afterSwap` (a hook's after-swap delta applies only to the unspecified currency, the token output of an exact-input buy), so a buy now reverts `PriceLimitedBuy` unless it carries the extreme bound `BUY_PRICE_LIMIT = 4295128740` (`TickMath.MIN_SQRT_PRICE + 1`, what v4 routers send for exact-input ETH -> token swaps), and `afterSwap` reverts `PartialFill` unless the pool consumed exactly the taxed input. Full-fill buys pay exactly as before; sells, which are charged in `afterSwap` on the ETH actually paid out, are unchanged and may carry any limit.

## Accepted in v3.18

- Accepted — a3749636 low, manager float: `beforeSwap` takes the buy tax (`manager.take`) from the PoolManager before the swapper settles its input, so the manager must hold at least that much ETH during the swap. The take is repaid in the same unlock by the hook's positive before-swap delta, which the swapper settles; the live Robinhood PoolManager holds about 24,500 ETH, far above any single buy's tax (a 10 ETH buy at 90% takes 9 ETH), and a manager without the float makes the buy revert rather than lose funds. Removing it would require holding the tax as ERC-6909 claims and a separate unlock in `flushFees`, a larger hook change not made for this fix. Accepted as is; the pattern is unchanged from every earlier version.
- Accepted — Exact-input swaps only (updated): V4Market rejects exact-output swaps and, since v3.18, any buy whose `sqrtPriceLimitX96` is not `BUY_PRICE_LIMIT`; routers and integrations request exact input at the extreme bound with a minimum output. A buy that cannot be filled in full at the extreme bound (liquidity exhausted) reverts `PartialFill` instead of paying tax on the unfilled part.

## Accepted at L4 (job B import audit, info)

- Accepted — 0d49094e info, `GridOperator._resolve` keeps a submitted plan's severity after the positive-streak override replaces its kind: when fewer than four Green/Cheap events occurred in the previous 19 epochs, `_resolve` replaces the plan's kind with Green or Cheap (`src/GridOperator.sol` line 222) but leaves `x.severity` at the submitted value (line 212), so a negative plan at the 3000 floor becomes a positive event at 3000 (a `FarmEngine` output bonus of severity / 10 = 300 bps) while an unsubmitted epoch in the same situation resolves positive at 10000 (1000 bps). The forced positive is weaker than the fallback positive by up to 700 bps for every local farm that epoch; no funds move outside the designed reward budget and the swarm cannot gain from it, since it only lowers everyone's positive event. Reproduced in `test/AuditL4V318.t.sol` against the pinned code. Not fixable: `GridOperator` is pinned in the bound L3 registry (`ModuleCode`), and the L4 launch deploys the exact recorded creation hash. Keeper/swarm rule: submit plans for forced-positive epochs (fewer than four positives in the trailing 19) at a high severity; the test shows a 10000 plan resolves at full strength.
